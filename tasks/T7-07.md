# T7-07  Reminders and expiry

## Inputs
- company.yaml > legal

## Outputs
- engine/cockpit/jobs/daily.js

## Steps
1. Daily: remind senders of documents SENT longer than unsigned_reminder_days.
2. Daily: notify the legal group expiry_notice_days before expires_on; set EXPIRED on documents past validity that were never executed.
3. Daily: filing_reminder actions due.
4. Every job logs one line to Automation logs and alerts after two consecutive failures.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- With a test clock, each reminder fires once on its day.
- A failing job alerts on the second failure.
