# T7-02  Approval hold and release

## Inputs
- engine/cockpit/approvals.js

## Outputs
- Document detail screen: approval panel

## Steps
1. On submit, evaluate rules; no match moves DRAFT to READY; a match moves it to IN_REVIEW and opens approvals.
2. EXTERNAL_PAPER documents always open an approval under the external_paper condition.
3. Approvers see the draft, the terms and the rule that applied.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- A PS-MNDA on the unchanged template goes straight to READY.
- An external-paper record cannot reach READY without an approval.

## Evidence
- setup-status.json entry with commit reference
