# T8-04  Joiner and leaver checklists

## Inputs
- defaults/checklists.yaml (to create)

## Outputs
- People view in Setup
- JoinerChecklists rows

## Steps
1. Define default joiner and leaver items in defaults/checklists.yaml: account, groups, policy acknowledgements, device check, agreements signed; leaver: suspend, transfer, revoke, recover devices.
2. Open a checklist from T5-01 and T5-03 and from the open_joiner_checklist action.
3. Items tied to data, such as acknowledgements and executed agreements, tick themselves.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- Adding a person opens a checklist; signing their PS-PIIA ticks the agreement item.

- setup-status.json entry with commit reference
