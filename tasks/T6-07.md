# T6-07  Carry-over for new pack versions

## Inputs
- Bindings of the superseded version

## Outputs
- engine/cockpit/carryover.js
- Node tests

## Steps
1. On intake of a new version, match each new bracket to the old version's bindings by text and normalised context; copy kind, key and field with confirmed true.
2. Unmatched brackets stay unconfirmed; removed brackets are listed.
3. Produce a change report: added, removed and changed brackets, and paragraphs whose text changed.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- A copy of PS-MNDA with one added bracket and one reworded paragraph carries over every other binding and reports exactly those two changes.

## Evidence
- setup-status.json entry with commit reference
