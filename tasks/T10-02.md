# T10-02  Publication and acknowledgement

## Inputs
- content/policies/

## Outputs
- command: box publish policies
- Acknowledge action in the cockpit

## Steps
1. On merge, render each changed policy to PDF in 60 Security and Compliance/Policies with its version.
2. The cockpit lists policies a person has not acknowledged at the current version; acknowledging writes an Events row.
3. A new version re-opens acknowledgement for everyone.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- A changed policy appears as a new PDF and re-opens acknowledgement.
- Acknowledgements are countable per person and version.

## Evidence
- setup-status.json entry with commit reference
