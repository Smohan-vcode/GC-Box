# T10-01  Policy templates and rendering

## Inputs
- defaults/policies.yaml

## Outputs
- defaults/policies/POL-01.md to POL-21.md
- command: box render policies

## Steps
1. Write each policy as a Markdown template of at most two pages: purpose, scope, rules, enforcement, owner role, review period. Company values only as placeholders.
2. Front matter: id, version, owner_role, review_months, enforcement (control ids or `manual`).
3. `box render policies` writes content/policies/ on first run and never overwrites an edited file; it reports templates that changed since.

## Human steps
- None.

## Exit criteria
- 21 templates pass `box check-literals`.
- Rendered policies contain no unresolved placeholder.
- Every policy names at least one control id or `manual`.

## Evidence
- setup-status.json entry with commit reference
